## Hideout / Inventory Management
General guidelines for managing inventory without requiring additional space.

* Keep ✔ (Found In Raid) for quests and specific hideout requirements.
* Sell what you can buy.
* Use **search** and prefer packing density over manual container organization.
* Minimize nesting to **1 level** of containers. Things requiring more than a
  click to find **will** be lost and should be reserved for **hideout** and
  quest items. Pack larger items into containers to allow most flexibility.
* Lock items not containers.
